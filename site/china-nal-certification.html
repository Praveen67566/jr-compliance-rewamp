NAL Certification - A Certificate For Telecom Equipment

Contact us - JR Compliance to obtain flexible and reliable NAL certification services to enter the Chinese market for telecom equipment. Reach  our experts to know how to get an NAL certification.

What is NAL Certification?

National Access License (NAL) is an obligatory certification requirement for telecommunication equipment in China. It is provided by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT), an authority which is also responsible for maintaining and interconnecting public telecommunication networks. 

Moreover, it includes 25 different types of product categories which approximately includes 300 different types of telecommunication devices. Moreover, it includes types of equipment - basic and high-end equipment, both the types of equipment required to go through testing to meet the test parameters.

In a nutshell, China NAL certification is required by cell phones and mobile wireless devices to sell or distribute products in the Chinese market.

Documents Required For NAL Certification

Business license of the applicant, manufacturer and factory
Commission processing contract
Applicants must be a domestic registered company. So if foreigners want to apply for NAL, the applicant can be their branch registered in China or other company whose registered business scope must include the after-sale and R&D capability
ISO Certificate of the factory
The company introduction for applicant, manufacturer and factory
Organization chart of the applicant, manufacturer and factory
Technical specification
External and Internal Photos
Authorization letter
Production and inspection equipment list
Test report

NAL Certification Process
