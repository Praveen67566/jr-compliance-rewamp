What is Annual Filing with ROC?

Companies incorporated under the Companies Act 1956 or the Companies Act 2013 must file the audited financial statement with ROC. 

In simpler words, it means a company must file the annual return form or audit financial statement to the ROC.

What is ROC | ROC full form

ROC full form is - Registrar of Company is the office that works under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), which controls the administration of every company and Limited Liability Partnership (LLP). Thus, MCA can control all the establishments and LLP that falls under the Companies Act 1956 or Companies Act 2013.

Moreover, in India, every company needs approval from ROC for incorporation. Thus, once an establishment files an application for incorporation, ROC will provide the certificate of Incorporation after scrutinization.

What is the penalty on non-filing of the annual return?

If a company fails to file the annual return on time, you must pay Rs 100 per day until the annual return filing is complete.

Benefits of Annual Filing With ROC

Legal Benefit

If a company fails to file the annual filing with ROC, a penalty would be imposed on the same. Thus, ROC filing must be completed before the due date to avoid legal issues. 

Determination of Financial Position

The annual filing includes the compilation of the company’s total accounts of a financial year. Thus, it assists in analyzing or determining the company’s financial position.

Serve as an Existence Proof

The regular filing of annual filing with ROC allows the government to keep a record of the company’s existence. On the contrary, if a company fails to do so, it won’t be considered ROC approved, which can be regarded as fake.

Pre-requisites to Complete When Filing ROC Return Form | Pre-requisites to Complete Annual Filing With ROC

If you don’t have a Director Identification Number (DIN) or Digital Signature Certificate (DSC), you must apply for the same. 

You must register the DSC of the authorized signatory or signatories on the official portal of MCA.

If there is an error in the Master Data of the company, you must rectify it from the respective ROC office. 

To fill the ROC return filing form, you must download the latest e-form and instruction kit. 

Understand the instructions before filling the form, and ensure proper internet connection when filing the form. 

For PDF conversion, you can use the PDF conversion provided on the MCA portal. Consequently, an excel or word file can be uploaded for conversion. To find the converted PFD, check your provided mail.

Systematic Process of Annual Filing For Companies | ROC Return Filing Process

Visit the official MCA portal and click on the “My Services tab.”

From the link “Downloads e-forms,” download the form as applicable. 

Further, provide your CIN number and click on “Pre-fill.” Consequently, some of the fields would fill automatically, such as the company’s name or address. 

Now, fill the rest of the fields and affix the documents as required. Though, the total size of the e-form, including attachments, must be above 2.5 MB.

Lastly, click on “Check Form.” On the contrary, if you have not provided any detail, you will be alert about the same. 

Fix the essential inaccuracies and click on “Signature Box,” affix the Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) by clicking on “Sign and save as.”

 At last, click on “Pre-scrutiny,” and your form will be locked, in case it is error-free.

Note- In case of any error in ROC return filing, you will get notified, which needs rectification. Click on “Modify” and follow the instructions as prescribed.

Have Questions? We've got answers.

What is Pre-scrutiny?

Pre-scrutiny is an option that you are required to choose when filling the e-form. After clicking on this option, the system will notify you in case your application includes any error. However, if not, it will lock the information.

What will happen if I do not pay ROC return form filing fee at any authorized bank branch?

If you fail to make the filing fee before the expiry period of the challan, your form must not be approved, and you are required to file the ROC form again. 

Can you do a revised filing of the already filed form?

Indeed, you can do a revised filing of the already filed form, excluding 23 AC or ACA and 23 AC-XBRL or 23 ACA-XBRL. Though, for the revised filing of the already filed form, you must pay the fee for the same. 

How can the defaulting status of the company be removed?

The company which has been marked as default must pay all the due annual and balance sheets to remove the defaulting status. 

Whether the defaulting status of the company is available on the MCA21 portal?

Indeed, you can see the year-wise default status while viewing the Master data of the company. 

Who should sign the annexure of the Director’s report?

According to Section 134 of the Companies Act, the same individual must sign the director’s report and annexure. 

How can I check the status of the Annual filing with ROC (e-form)?

To check the status of the annual e-form, you can visit the official MCA website.
