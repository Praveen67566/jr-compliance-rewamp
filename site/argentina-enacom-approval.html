We are an action-oriented consultancy firm, who believe in providing 100% assistance to our clients by investing our sustain efforts in completing the Argentina ENACOM certification immediately. 

To know more about ENACOM Type Approval and its process, contact us immediately.

What is ENACOM?

Ente Nacional de Comunicaciones (ENACOM) is an authority which is responsible for providing certification to manufacturers who are engaged in manufacturing devices which use radio frequencies. 

As a matter of fact, radio frequencies are not good for the final consumer, yet, with development, it is considered necessary to improvised these devices. Thus, more exposure to the radio frequencies. However, Argentina’s government has implemented certain standards which are obligatory to follow. 

Argentina ENACOM certification is obligatory to enter into the Argentina market. In  nutshell, without ENACOM type approval certification, a product can not sell or distribute in the market. 

Moreover, only obtaining ENACOM type approval is not enough; you are also required to visibly apply its official logo on your product to reflect the conformity of the product with the established guidelines.

What is the validity of ENACOM certification?

Argentina ENACOM certification is valid for three (3) years.

Where should you implement the logo of the ENACOM?

It must be labeled on the product. However, if you can not apply it on the product, then it should be printed on the user manual or package of the product.
