What is ISO Certification?

International Standard Organization (ISO) certification is the quality management tool used to provide the identity to the product to reflect the company’s quality management system. Thus, it promotes worldwide standardization of specifications and requirements for products, materials, formats, procedures, information, and quality management.

In short, ISO certification indicates that a product, material, etc., has complied with the requirements and specifications. Hence, it is a guarantee that ISO standards concerning the requirement and management has been completed. 

Moreover, it is an independent organization with authority to provide standards regarding the quality and safety of a product.

Is an ISO certificate relevant for a small business or startup with below 15 employees?

Indeed, the revised version of ISO is relevant for small and large organizations, which will assist you in business diversification and improve customer satisfaction.

What are ISO certification bodies?

ISO certification bodies are organizations with authority to issue ISO certificates to the establishments complying with ISO certification standards.

Pre-requisites to ISO Certification

1. Choose the Appropriate Certificate Type | ISO Certification Types

First, you are required to choose the appropriate type of ISO certification for your establishment because there are various types of ISO certificates available, 

  • ISO 9001 2008 (for quality management)
  • ISO 27001 (for information security management)
  • ISO 14001 (for environmental management)
  • ISO 22008 (for food safety management)
  • ISO 9001: 2015 (for quality management systems)
  • ISO 29990: 2010 (for non-formal education and training learning services)
  • ISO 15001: 2010 (for anesthetic management and respiratory equipment)
  • ISO 16949: 2009 (for QMS for automotive production and relevant service part)
  • HACCP (for Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point)
  • ISO 10012: 2003 (for measurement management system)
  • ISO 27001: 2013 (for information security management services)
  • ISO 31000: 2018 (for risk management)
  • ISO 20121: 2012 (for event sustainability management system)
  • ISO 45001: 2018 (for occupational health and safety management system)
  • ISO 2000-1: 2006 (for information technology)
  • ISO 50001: 2011/2015 (for energy management system)
  • CE Mark (implies that a product complies with the European standards)
  • ISO 19011: 2011 (for guidelines for auditing management system)
  • ISO 22000:2018 (for food safety management system (FSMS))
  • HALAL (food items is permissible for consumption and used by the Muslims)
  • ISO 4217: 2015 (applies on the representation of currencies)
  • ISO 13485: 2015 (for medical devices QMS) 
  • ISO 16732-1: 2012 (for fire safety engineering)

2. Choose the Appropriate ISO Body


ISO only creates international standards. However, it is not engaged in ISO certification. In simpler words, external certification bodies perform the function of providing ISO certification. 

Yet, while choosing the appropriate body for ISO certification, keep the below-mentioned points insight,

  • Evaluate various ISO service providers or ISO certification consultant.
  • Check whether the ISO service provider is accredited or not. Though, being accredited is not mandatory and does not signify the service provider’s reputation. But, importantly, it should meet the requirements of the standard. 
  • CASCO is an authority that publishes policy and develops standards. Thus, you must check whether certification bodies follow appropriate CASCO standards or not.

Process of ISO Certificate Online | Apply ISO Online | ISO Certificate Registration (ISO Certificate Online) Process

The applicant will enter into a contract with the registrar, which signifies the rights and obligations of both parties for ISO Certificate Online. 

ISO appointed auditors will review the quality manuals and documents concerning various policies and procedures followed by the establishment. This will assist the auditor in estimating the potential gaps against the prescribed ISO requirements.

Further, an action plan needs to be prepared to eradicate the gaps. Moreover, employees will require training to adopt the new procedures to improve efficiency. In simpler words, every employee must posses the knowledge regarding the ISO standards and policies. 

 Then, there would be auditing in two stages. In stage-1, the ISO auditor will check the changes made by the applicant to find the potential non-conformities in the system and procedures. Then, an auditor will categorize the minor and major non-conformities. In stage-2, the auditor will complete the auditing for the final time to check whether all the non-conformities are eradicated or not to move the ISO Certificate Online forward. 

Once a product satisfies the ISO standards, a final report will be made and forward to the registrar. 

Lastly, if your product withstands the ISO standards and requirements, a certificate will be obtained. 

Note- A surveillance audit will be conducted from time to time to ensure that an organization complies with the ISO standards.

Who can apply for ISO 9001 standards?

It applies to a company, irrespective of its size or sector. So, for example, it could be a Partnership, Proprietorship firm, One Person Company (OPC), Public Limited Company, Trust, NGO, etc.

How to implement the requirements of 9001 in a business?

Learn and understand the requirements and vocabulary related to the IS/ISI 9001 or IS/ISO 9000. Along with the directions related to the IS/ISO 9004. Then, implement the various requirements in activities and processes. 

Is ISO 9000 certification applicable to the service sector?

Indeed, ISO 9000 certification applies to the service sector. 

How to display your certificate on a product?

When displaying the label on a product, don’t write “ISO certified” or “ISO certification,” but say, “ISO 9001: 2015 certification,” as per the type of ISO certificate you have chosen. 

What is the validity duration of the ISO certification?

ISO certificate is valid for three years.
