Serviceâ Overview

The Telecommunication Engineering Center (STQC) is the authority that issues the Software Product Certification for telecommunication equipment. This body comes under the Telecom Commission and Nodal Agencies, viz., the Department of Telecommunication, the Ministry of Communications, and Information Technology, which hold the authority to generate standards and requirements along with specifications of the telecom products, networks, and services.

Benefits

Legal Authorization for Market Entry

Software Product Certification allows businesses to legally sell telecom products in the Indian market.

Better Product Credibility

The STQC certified products are trusted by the customers and trade about attaining very high safety and performance norms.

No Legal Penalty

Compliance with STQC rules offers smooth operations without legal hoops, keeping businesses away from expensive penalties.

Edge In The Market

Software Product Certification places the business above other telecom operators, indicating reliability of products after being tested against the norms laid by the regulatory authorities.

Types of STQC Approvals

STQC Type Approval
Issued for telecom products meeting Generic Requirement (GR) standards, ensuring safety, quality, and performance compliance before market deployment.
STQC Interface Approval
Certifies that telecom equipment meets Interface Requirements (IR) for seamless integration with existing network infrastructure, ensuring interoperability.
Service Provider Approval
Coverage for operation as a telecom service provider (TSP) is to certify the lawfulness of the operation of the service provider, proving that it meets the regulatory, technical, and security requirements.
CoA
Issued for telecom products that are neither GR nor IR but require approvals for market entry, considering safety and functionality.
Technology Approval
Evaluates new telecom technologies for compliance, security, and compatibility with Indian telecom standards, fostering innovation in the sector.

04

FAQ

STQC

FAQ

Why is Software Product Certification mandatory for telecom devices?
Software Product Certification ensures that telecom equipment satisfies national safety and technical standards. Without it, businesses cannot legally sell or import telecom products in India. It also helps prevent network disruptions, security breaches, and performance issues.
What is the time frame for obtaining Software Product Certification?
Software Product Certification application processing time may take from weeks to months as the process depends on the product test results, document accuracy, and approvals received from relevant regulatory sectors. Working with Software Product Certification experts helps hasten the process and avoid delays.
Do international manufacturers also apply for Software Product Certification?
Yes, Software Product Certification is applicable for both Indian and foreign manufacturers to sell telecom equipment in India. Foreign companies must have an authorized representative in India who will coordinate compliance and certification formalities.
What if a product fails STQC testing?
In case a product fails the testing, manufacturers must implement the required corrections and perform retesting. Any such actions may lead to delays and additional costs, which is why pre-compliance testing is always recommended to ensure the product meets STQC standards before submission.
Is Software Product Certification renewed?
No, Software Product Certification is usually granted as a one-time approval for a specific model, unless the product undergoes major modifications to affect its compliance standards. However, manufacturers must keep updated with any regulatory changes that may require retesting or further approval.
