What is VCCI Certification?

Voluntary Control Council for Interference (VCCI) by Information Technology Equipment indicates that a product has utilizes the low electromagnetic emission. VCCI certification or mark serves as proof that a product has met the prescribed standards through testing. 

The fundamental reason for obtaining the VCCI certification is to encourage the voluntary control of radio disturbances emitted from the Information Technology Equipment (ITE). While apart from, it also ensures the safety of the final consumer.

VCCI Certification Process

Product testing will be conducted in an accredited laboratory.

The application will be duly filed.

Verification report (product test report) needs to be submitted with the application.

If conformity requirements have been met, a VCCI certificate will be issued.

Thereafter, VCCI mark can be implemented on the products.
