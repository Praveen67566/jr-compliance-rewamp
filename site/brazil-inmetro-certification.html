INMETRO Certification - A Certificate of Conformity

INMETRO certification is an obligatory requirement or a passageway to enter the Brazilian market as it indicates that a product has met the safety requirements - thus, safe for the consumers.

Contact our experts for reliable INMETRO certification services.

What is INMETRO Certification?

It is an accreditation body which is responsible for providing accreditation of each product Certification Body (CB). Also, different requirements are applicable on particular products depending on the certification scheme. 

In a nutshell, a certificate is obligatory to obtain CB certification to sell or distribute products in the Brazilian market. 

Obtaining INMETRO certificate is mandatory to enter the Brazilian market, however, it is not easy as it seems but it includes understanding of applicable standard on a product, documentation, knowledge of requirements to be followed, and more.

INMETRO Certification Process

An application will be duly filed.

Product testing will be conducted to analyze whether a product has met the requirements or not.

To ensure an appropriate production process is followed, factory inspection and assessment will be conducted.

Thereafter, technical analysis of documents will be done.

If factor inspection and product has met the established standard, a certificate will be issued.
