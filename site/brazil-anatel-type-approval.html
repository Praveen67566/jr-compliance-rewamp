ANATEL Type Approval - An Obligatory Requirement For Telecom Equipment

To sell or distribute telecommunication equipment in Brazil - ANATEL type approval is basic plus obligatory requirement without which a product can not be legally sold or distributed. If you are looking for a reliable consultants to provide you assistance in completing the ANATEL type approval - you are at right place.

What is ANATEL Type Approval?

Agência Nacional de Telecomunicações (ANATEL) certification is an authority which regulates telecommunication equipment. Like INMETRO certification, it is obligatory to obtain the ANATEL certification to set a foot in the Brazilian market. 

In brief, without ANATEL type approval it is legally not possible to sell or distribute telecommunication equipment in Brazil. 

ANATEL includes three different categories to classify the telecommunication product-

  • Category I includes the products made for final consumers such as mobile phones.
  • Category II includes the products which use Radio Frequency (RF) such as radio broadcasting equipment.
  • Category III includes products such as cable connectors.

ANATEL Certification Process

Application will be duly filled.

A contract will be signed with the Designated Certification Body (OCD) after reviewing the specs to determine the test requirements. 

Products will be submitted for testing in an accredited laboratory.

After conducting appropriate testing, the product testing report will be analyzed or reviewed by the OCD.

A Certificate of Conformity will be issued by OCD.

Thereafter, the documentation will be reviewed by the ANATEL to issue the Certificate of Homologation.
