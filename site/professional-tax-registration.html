What is Professional Tax?

A professional tax applicable under Article 276 is an indirect tax that the State governments collect from the individual who is practicing a profession such as a lawyer, chartered accountant, doctor, company secretary, and more.

What is Professional Tax Registration?

Professional tax registration is simply a registration process within 30 days of employing the staff in a business. Moreover, if the work location differs, you must submit the individual registration application per the jurisdiction.

Moreover, Professional tax registration online process make it effortless to complete the registration process.

Will there be a penalty if the Professional Tax registration is delayed?

In case of a delay in registration, you must pay Rs 5 per day as a penalty. Moreover, in case of non-payment or delay in payment, you are liable to pay 10% of the tax penalty.

Additionally, in case of late filing of returns, you need to pay a penalty of Rs 300.

Note - Penalty concerning the Professional Tax Rate could vary depending on States.

Benefits of Paying Professional Tax

Legal Benefit

According to the rules and regulations, every employee is obligated to make a professional tax payment. However, failing can result in penalties that increase with the duration of not paying tax.

Thus, after professional tax registration, an employee has to pay the tax to the respective State government.

Deductions Can be Claimed

Based on professional tax paid, you can claim the deductions in the salary. However, the deductions can only be claimed year corresponding to which the taxpayer makes the payment.

Professional Tax Registration Online Process

  • Visit the official website of APPT registration.
  • Through email registration, generate your password, which will allow you to log in.
  • As you generate the password after verifying your email, log in to commence the registration process.
  • As you will click on the “New Registration” provide the below-mentioned details -
  1. If you have, enter the TIN or ETIN, or LTTIN (anyone).
  2. Provide the application date in the format of DDMMYYYY.
  3. Providing details of EDR.
  4. Select the circle from the list (mandatory).
  5. Enter the details of category type, under which provide the company’s name.
  6. You might be required to provide the PAN of the individual and company.
  7. Further, enter the details of the organization’s address such as door number, street name or number, locality, name of city/town/village, and municipality or Mandal.
  • You will enter details of the district, PIN code, mobile number, etc.
  • Also, include details of Service Tax Number and Annual turnover.
  • At last, after entering the number of employees, click on “Next Step.”
  • Now, a reference number will appear on the screen (note it for further reference).
  • Lastly, click “ok.”


Note- As you enter the “OK,” you are required to complete the data entry and enter the details as needed.
