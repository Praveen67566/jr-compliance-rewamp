What is CE Certification?

Conformité Européenne (CE) certification or mark indicates that a product meets European directive standards, thus ensuring the safety and health of the consumer. It does not signify the product quality, but it demonstrates that a product withstands the standards to be sold in the European market. 

Moreover, CE certificate or mark does not illustrate the production of a product in the European Economic Area (EEA). However, it signifies that a product complies with the European standards, and can be sold in the European market. 

Thus, it is an obligation that a manufacturer or its agents, including the manufacturer who desires to import goods must fulfill.

When is CE certification compulsory?

CE certification is compulsory for the products on which EU requirements apply. Thus, affixing the CE mark on a product ensures whether you need certification for the same or not, because it is restricted to affix the CE mark on the product which is not obligated to follow EU specifications.

Benefits of CE Certification

Legal Advantage

As mentioned, without CE certification your product can not access the EEA market. However, the enforcement could differ, some companies also forage CE stamps or disregard CE compliance, which can result in legal action against a company. 

The penalty could include fines, imprisonment, product ban, and product recalls, which would be more expensive than obtaining the CE certificate legally because it not only affects you financially but affects business goodwill too.

Therefore, to avoid legal penalties, obtain the CE certification India.

Wider Market Access

A CE mark ensures easy market access to the product in the European market because it ensures that a product has complied with the safety requirement. Moreover, it would also build the customer's confidence in the product.

Pre-requisites to Follow Before Obtaining CE Certification

  • You must comply with the EU directives or regulations. 
  • Ascertain whether you need a notified body or you can assess your product yourself.
  • Compile a technical report for document conformity. 
  • Draft and sign the EU conformity declaration.

Note- A manufacturer does not require a license to affix the CE mark on a product, he just has to declare conformity with all requirements. 

How to Create Technical Documentation?

  • Personal information such as your name, address, or of any authorized representative.
  • A short product description.
  • List of parts a product includes.
  • Product identification, such as its serial number.
  • Name and address of the facilities which are engaged in product designing and manufacturing. 
  • The name and address of the notified body engaged in product conformity assessment.
  • The statement regarding the followed conformity assessment procedure. 
  • A statement demonstrating the relevant regulations a product needs to comply with.
  • Label and instruction of use.
  • An EU conformity declaration.

How to Get CE Certificate?

  • Determine whether a product covers under the CE directive or not. If it does, the CE certificate needs to be obtained.
  • After discovering the essential requirements in the directives applicable to a product, it will be  find out whether a product complies with these requirements or not. Also it needs to be discover, a product needs a conformity assessment body test or not.  
  • Now, check if third-party assessment will be required or not, as specific directives need to be tested on the product by the third-party organization to ensure that it meets the conformity requirements. 
  • Finally, access to the product conformity, for which there are two approaches,

1. Conformity assessment by the manufacturer: Here, a manufacturer will hire a compliance service provider to avail of test facilities. However, for this, the manufacturer has to make payment.

2. Conformity assessment by a Notified Bodies (NB): Likewise, here the NB will be used for the conformity assessment and to certify its products.

  • Lastly, technical documentation will be prepared.
  • The document that complies with the CE certificate directives is the Declaration of Conformity which demonstrates that the manufacturer is responsible to obey the applicable directives on the product.

How to Affix the CE Mark?

The CE mark should be appropriately visible, ineffaceable, and readable. 

CE mark must include initials, “CE” in the same vertical dimensions and its size must not be below 5mm unless specified. 

In case you desire to change the size of the CE mark, keep the proportions of two letters in insight.

If your product has to comply with various EC regulations or directives to obtain a CE mark or certification, the product’s accompanying document should demonstrate that a product complies with every EC directive or regulation. 

In case CE marking can not be affixed on the product, you must affix it on its package or any documents.
