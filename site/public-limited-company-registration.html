What is a Public Limited Company?

Under Companies Act, 2013, a Public Limited Company refers to a company with limited liability and provides its shares liberally to the general public. Moreover, the stock of the Public Limited Company can be acquired by any individual, either privately or through trades.

Also, to form a Public Limited Company a company should have at least three directors, seven shareholders, and a maximum fifty directors, including the minimum paid up capital of Rs 5 lakhs. 

A Public limited company enjoys the benefits of a private limited company Registation.

Registration of a Public limited company is as important as that of private limited company because it offers a number of benefits like ease to transfer the shares, more transparency, easy access to loans or government tenders, and more. 

Though, it is a well known fact that a public limited company is governed much more strictly as compared to the private limited company. Therefore, for a layman it is tough to take care of registration on his own.

Major Benefits of a Public Limited Company

Disperse the Risk

As a public limited company, it sells shares to the larger part of the general public, which helps in dispersing the unsystematic risk. Thus, reducing the fear of facing the risk alone by a shareholder. 

Limited Liability

Limited liability is one of the finest features of the public limited company as it does not impose the company's debt on its members. In other words, the member of the company is not liable to pay the debts or loss of the company by selling their personal assets for the amount greater than their invested capital. 

Easy Access to Loans

It is easy for the public limited company to obtain loans hassle-free , which might be tough to obtain in case of a private limited company. Including, if the public limited company is registered under the prescribed provisions, obtaining from financial institutions and banks will become more feasible. 

More Capital and Better Opportunities

As mentioned, a public limited company  offers its shares to the general public in larger quantities, which indicates that any individual can invest in a public company. That provides an opportunity to raise more capital. 

In addition to which, listing of public limited companies on a stock market, provides more and better business opportunities to a company to grow and diversify.

What Are the Basic Requirements For Public Limited Company Registration?

For incorporation of a public limited company, at least seven shareholders must be required.
Three directors are the minimum requirement to form a public limited company.
A minimum share capital of Rs 5 lakhs.
Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) of at least one director will be required during the submission of self-attested copies to the authorities.
Digital Identification Number of the Directors will be required.
An application will be required in regard to the selection of the company’s name.
Documents like Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA), including Form DIR-12, INC-7, and INC-22 must be required.
An application signifying the main object clause of the company.

Documents Required For Public Limited Company Registration

Identity proof of shareholders and directors.
Address proof of the shareholders and directors.
PAN number of each shareholder and director.
Utility bill of the registered office.
If required, No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the owner of the office.
DIN and DSC of directors.
Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA).

How to Start Public Limited Company in India?

Check the availability of the name of the company.

Once the company's will be finalized, an application for a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) and Director Identification Number (DIN) will be filed.

Then, an application for Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Article of Association (AoA) needs to be filed.

Thereafter, an application for the PAN and TAN of the company will be filed.

With PAN and TAN, the certificate of incorporation will be issued.

Lastly, on the name of the company a current account will be opened.

Moreover, the application form will be submitted along with the required documents.
