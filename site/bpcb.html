Consistent growth in different areas of the industry are consistently increasing the pollution. That's why different states are obligatory to follow the CPCB's established guidelines. Likewise, Bihar State Pollution Control Board has implemented several laws to control or prevent pollution in different sectors.

What is Bihar State Pollution Control Board?

Bihar State Pollution Control Board (BSPCB) was established in 1974 under the provisions of Water Act, 1974. It has been established as a regulatory body for the implementation of the pollution control laws. 

In a nutshell, similar to other State Pollution Control Authority or Committee such as DPCC, MPCB, or UPPCB, etc are committed to provide a safe environment by ensuring appropriate implementation of environmental laws, which has been established by the concerned authorities.

Additionallly, to uniformly implement the environmental laws throughout the State with the purpose to keep environmental issues under several necessary steps have been taken by the Government of Bihar to control the pollution through the proper implementation of laws.

Major Functions of BSPCB

Developing programmes to prevent and control the pollution.
Providing adequate advice to the government in regard to pollution issues.
To collect and disseminate the information to prevent pollution.
Participate and conduct Research and Development to prevent pollution.
Organize and train mass awareness programmes in collaboration with the Central Board.
Inspect and review the treatment plant.
Establishing standards for sewage and effluent treatment.
Establishing appropriate orders for the prevention and control of discharges of effluent or construction systems.
Providing advice to the State government in regard to industrial location.
Performing all the functions which have been established by CPCB.
Establishing or recognizing the laboratories to discharge effluent analysis.

Categorization of Industries

Under BSPCB industries have been categorized into red, orange, green, and white categories. The industrial categorization has been done primarily on the basis of industrial size and consumption. 

For clearly understanding, check below-mentioned table -

  • Red category industries have pollution index scores of 60 and above.
  • Orange category industries have pollution index scores of 41 to 59.
  • Green category industries have pollution index scores of 21 to 40.
  • White category industries have pollution index scores of incl. And upto 20.

To be precise, Bihar in total has 293 industries which has been classified in 64 industries falling under Red category in Bihar, 94 under orange category, 75 under Green and 60 under white category. 

So, understanding under which category your industry falls is essential to move forward to how to obtain a CTE under BSPCB. Now that we have discussed industrial categorization, let’s see what are the steps which you are required to take to obtain a CTE/CTO.

Documents Required to Obtain CTE/CTO Under BSPCB

Documents to Obtain CTE -

PAN card or Aadhar card or Voter ID.

Sale deed, lease deed, land receipt, and other land documents.

Project reports such as sum cost of project, list of plants and machinery, etc.

Location map.

Replica of environmental clearance from concerned authority.

Undertaking in respect to the distance of clearance landmark from boundary.

Documents To Obtain CTO -

PAN card or Aadhar card or Voter ID.

Replica of previous CTE/ CTO.

Replica of environmental clearance along with compliance report.

Undertaking in regard to compliance of CTE/ CTO conditions.

How to Obtain CTE/CTO Under BSPCB?

An applicant will be registered on the official website.

Once registration will be done, CTE will be filed along with the required documents. 

Once the document submission is done, the requisite fee will be paid and form will be submitted.

If required, the application will be scrutinized by the Assistance and Junior Environmental Engineer (JEE). 

Thereafter, JEE will communicate the recommendations with the Member Secretary. 

For the approval of the chairman, the Member Secretary will forward his recommendation to the Chairman.

Once the Chairman provides approval, a NOC/ CTE will be issued by the Member Secretary which will send the ID of the applicant.
