What is CCC Certification?

China Compulsory Certificate (CCC) is the certification process that allows the seller to affix a (China Compulsory Certificate) CCC mark China on the product to demonstrate that the product is safe. Thus, the (China Compulsory Certificate) CCC mark China is the passageway to the Chinese market. 

In December 2001,  the General Administration of Quality Supervision, Inspection, and Quarantine of the People's Republic of China (AQSIQ) and the Certification Accreditation Administration of the People's Republic of China publicized the CCC/ CCC certificate China (China Compulsory Certificate).

To be noted, CCC certification/ CCC certificate China was implemented on May 1, 2002. However, it came into full effect on August 1, 2003. 

Moreover, CCC certification incorporates China’s two mandatory inspection systems: CCIB (China Commodity Inspection Bureau) and CCEE (China Commission for Conformity Certification of Electrical Equipment) to simplify foreign trade. 

Additionally, from 2019 for certain products, you can either do the voluntary certification or (China Compulsory Certificate) CCC-self certification.

How to Get CCC Certificate | Process to Obtain CCC Certificate China

Before commencing the CCC certification, it will be determined whether the CCC certification is applied to a product or not. 

If it does, the CCC certification application form will be filled. 

After application submission, the test samples for testing needs to be provided to the CCC accredited laboratory. 

Along with testing, CCC certification officials will also conduct the initial inspection of the factory. 

If a products withstand the CCC certificate requirements, the CCC certification will be obtained. 

Note- To maintain the CCC certification, annual audits will be conducted.

Have questions? We've got answers.

Who administers the CCC certification?

The CCC certification is administered by the Certification and Accreditation Administration of the People's Republic of China (CNCA). 

What is the aim of CCC certification?

The objective of the CCC certificate is to provide safe products to its final customers. 

How to renew the CCC certification?

The CCC certification can only be renewed through a follow-up certification. 

What is the validity of the CCC certification?

The validity of the CCC certification is five years. However, it should be maintained through annual audits. 

How to know whether a product falls under CCC certification?

You can refer to the First Catalogue of Products Subject to China Compulsory Certification to find whether your product needs CCC certification or not. 

Will there be any penalty for not obtaining the CCC certification?

Indeed, the penalty would be imposed on the seller or importer if they try to sell a product in the Chinese market without approval. Moreover, the imported products would be returned to the senders, if not CCC marked. 

Thus, to enter your product in the Chinese market, you must obtain the CCC certificate.

Can I test a product outside China to obtain CCC certification?

No, the product testing outside China is not authorized.

Do I need additional certificates, even after obtaining CCC certificates?

Indeed, a product could require additional certifications. For instance, telecommunication products and medical products do not require additional certification. However, television might need to acquire a license from the SARFT. 

Thus, you must check the requirement of additional certification, as per the type of your product.

Mention the list of certification bodies and accreditation product categories?

Below-mention are some certification bodies and product categories, 

  • CCCF includes firefighting equipment.
  • CQC covers nearly all
  • CCC products, excluding agriculture machinery and firefighting equipment.
  • CCRC covers information technology equipment, a few household appliances, telecom, and audio or video.
  • CCAP covers every vehicle and automotive part.
