All charitable trusts, organisations, and Section 8 corporations that accept donations or gifts from abroad must apply for FCRA registration. The 2010 Foreign Contribution Regulation Act governs FCRA registration. FCRA stands for Foreign Contribution Regulation Act.

JR Compliance guarantee to provide you 100% assistance in registration of FCRA by providing hassle-free FCRA registration service to our clients. Contact our FCRA registration consultants to know how to apply for an FCRA certificate.

What is FCRA Certificate? | FCRA Full Form

Below-mentioned is the FCRA full form -

Foreign Contribution Regulation Act (FCRA) is a law implemented by the Ministry of Home Affairs to ensure that foreign contribution does not unfavourably affect security. Additionally, FCRA was first introduced in 1976, however, it was modified in 2010 when various new measures were introduced to control the foreign donations.

Moreover, FCRA applies to every NGO, groups, and associations to receive foreign donations. Thus, under FCRA, such NGOs need to obtain registration.

Whether the certificate of FCRA registration will be renewed?

According to Section 16 of FCRA, 2010, any individual who has obtained the registration certificate under Section 12 requires to renew the registration certificate within six months of the expiration of the duration of the certificate.

What would be the consequence of not renewing the certificate?

If an establishment fails to renew the certificate, you are required to apply for a fresh application for the grant of registration.

How to Apply For FCRA Certificate | FCRA Online Registration Process

Visit the official portal of the Ministry of Home Affairs.

Click on the “FCRA online firms.”

 As you move to the next page, click on “application for FCRA registration (FC-3).”

After clicking on that, you will move to the next screen and click on “click to apply online.”

If you are a new user, sign up to create an account.

After you click on “signup,” fill in information such as Darpan ID and details of Chief Functionary.

As you will fill in the details, note down your user ID.

Now, fill in the login details to move the process forward.

As you log in, a letter will be displayed on the screen where you have to choose FCRA registration under “I am applying for.” Then select “apply online.”

Further proceed with the application by filling in all the required information such as Associate details, executive committee, EC details, and other bank details. 

Lastly, as you are done filling in all the details, submit the application by affixing documents.

At last, make the online payment and keep the acknowledgment for future reference.

What could be the purpose of receiving foreign contributions?

An association registered under FCRA can receive foreign contributions for below-mention purposes, 

  • Social purpose
  • Religious purpose
  • Educational purpose
  • Cultural purpose
  • Economic purpose
To Whom FCRA,2010 Applicable?
  • Indian citizens residing in foreign.
  • Associate branches/subsidiaries in foreign countries, registered/incorporated in India.
  • Entire India.
When is the Registration of FCRA online Suspends?
  • On inspection of accounts and receiving any adverse input as opposed to functioning, the Ministry of Home Affairs can initially suspend the registration for 180 days.
  • The Ministry of Home Affairs can cancel the registration of an establishment that is not eligible for registration/grant of “prior permission” for three years from the date of cancellation.
Can the government cancel the registration certificate granted to an individual under FCRA?

Indeed, the government holds the authority to cancel the license according to Section 14 of FCRA, 2010. However, if the government cancels the licenses, then you are not eligible to obtain them for a certain duration of three years from the date of cancellation.
