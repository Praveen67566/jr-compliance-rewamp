USA Certification Services

Government of USA has established certain guidelines for the safety of the final consumer, that's why it is necessary to follow the certification requirements to prevent the potential injury or harmful effects of using an equipment. Moreover, obtaining certain certification such as FCC for electric equipment is obligatory to sell or import product in USA, likewise, there are other certificates which are mandatory to obtain. 

We - JR Compliance have an intregated approach to meet your compliance certification requirements within your time contraints. 

Federal Communications Commission 

(FCC)

The Federal Communications Commission (FCC) is an authority established to develop, enforce, and implement the regulations set by Congress under the Communications Act,1934, and the Telecommunications Act, 1996.

learn more

Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory (NRTL)

A Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory (NRTL) is an independent organization which certifies that a product meets the established standards, thus, can be sold or distributed in the USA.
