China Certification Services

Government of China has established certain guidelines for the safety of the final consumer to allievate the potential injury or harmful effects of using an equipment. That's why certificates such as China Compulsory (CCC), MIIT Network Access License, and CMIIT Radio Type Approval are introduced for electric, telecommunication, and wireless equipment.

JR Compliance - with the experience of 8+ years is equipped with all the resources to take care of each aspect of the certification.

China Compulsory Certification (CCC)

China Compulsory Certificate (CCC) is the certification process that allows the seller to affix a CCC mark on the product to demonstrate that the product is safe. Thus, the CCC mark is the passageway to the Chinese market. 

learn more

State Radio Regulatory Commission (SRRC)

State Radio Regulatory Commission (SRRC) is an authority which is responsible for providing certificates to the appliances and equipment which have radio transmitting functions. 

learn more

National Access License (NAL)

National Access License (NAL) is an obligatory certification requirement for telecommunication equipment in China. It is provided by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT), an authority which is also responsible for maintaining and interconnecting public telecommunication networks. 

learn more

China Energy Level (CEL)

The China Energy Level (CEL) refers to the energy consumption label implemented on the Chinese products. Moreover, every product utilizing electricity needs to have a CEL label implemented on the product to reflect that a product is energy efficient. 

learn more
