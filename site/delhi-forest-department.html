Overview of Delhi Forest Department

The constantly changing lifestyle is leading to urbanization. However, the dependence on natural resources is undeniable, as is the side effect of urbanization. 

The sustainable use of natural resources seems to be a perfect solution to this issue, which is why Delhi Forest Department seeks to preserve the forest in an attempt to secure the natural resources.

To achieve the above mentioned objective, according to National Forest Policy, 1988 at least 1-3rd of the areas of a state must be covered under forest. While the area covered under hills and mountainous areas is 2-3rd, this has been done to minimise soil erosion, land-degradation, and to ensure stability of the ecosystem.

Process For Granting of Permission Under DPTA

An application will be duly submitted.

Initial scrutiny will be done by a Tree officer.

Thereafter, a Tree officer will allocate an inspecting officer to examine the application.

An inspecting officer then will allot an inspecting date for assessment.

The report will be submitted by the inspecting officer.

If applicable, the Tree officer will submit the report to the higher authority.

As applicable, the application will be rejected or in-principle approval will be given.

If an in-principle approval is given, fee payment will be done.

Once the payment has been made, final permission will be given by the Tree officer.
