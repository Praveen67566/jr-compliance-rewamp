Telepermit - Comply With Telecom Standards of New Zealand

Be a part of flourishing New Zealand telecommunication equipment market by obtaining Telepermit certificate under the guidance of JR Compliance - a leading compliance service provider.

An Overview

It is applicable on telecommunication equipment which utilizes public networks. Without this certificate, a telecommunication equipment can not enter the New Zealand market as a product needs to meet the established technical standards. Thus, a certificate will be issued on the basis of test reports. 

Challenges in Telepermit Certificate

Identification and meeting the specifications or standards applicable on a product.
Coordinating with accredited testing laboratory for product testing.
Inaccurate/ improper knowledge of complex certification requirements applicable on product of particular product category and type.
Not availability in-country representative and representation for conformance with compliance.
Inaccurate submission of documents and forms.
Issues in custom clearance of sample products for product testing.
Non-conformance with queries/ issues raised by authorities.
