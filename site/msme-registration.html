What is MSME Registration?

Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprise (MSME) is the backbone of countries like India because these are the primary contributors to the socio-economic development of the country.

Statistically, India constitutes approximately 6.3 crores MSMEs. Also, the registration of MSME has increased by 18.5% year-over-year (Y-o-Y). Along with this, this sector contributes approximately 29% of GDP by national and international trade.

Additionally, MSME is also known as Small Scale Industry (SSI) and online MSME certificate is termed as UdyogAadhar or LaghuUdyog. Under the online SSI registration, the enterprise entities have classified into various categories as micro/small/medium depending on their investment in a manufacturing business or in a service sector.

Is MSME certificate mandatory?

It is advisable to obtain the MSME certificate to acquire the benefits which come with it.

What is the main benefit of MSME certificate?

The fundamental benefit of MSME registration online is that it provides recognition to the business through a unique number (which is given to a business).

Apart from this, with the growth of the MSME sector, there would be economic growth and increased employment.

Benefit of MSME Registration

Provide loans at a lower rate of interest.

Enable you to avail the benefits of the government schemes.

In the initial years of the commencement of the business, an MSME certificate provides you tax benefits concerning central excise and direct taxes.

Provides Industrial Promotion Rate subsidy eligibility.

Lessen the cost of trademark and patent registration. 

Avail the benefit in case of sales tax and other subsidies provided by the government. 

Categories of MSMEs

1. Manufacturing Enterprises

As the name implies, it comprises the enterprises which are engaged in manufacturing of the products which are mentioned in Schedule I of the Industrial Act, 1951. The manufacturing enterprises are described in terms of investment in Plant and Machinery. 

2. Service Enterprises

These are those enterprises that are engaged in providing the services. Also, these are enterprises described in terms of investment as equipment. 

Documents Required For MSME Registration

The requirement of documents for online MSME registration online is different depending on the type of firm. 

Proprietary Firm

  • Address proof of the company.
  • Certificate of shops and establishments.

Private limited company or OPC

  • PAN of company.
  • Address of company.
  • Certificate of a corporation.
  • Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Article of Association (AOA).

LLP or Partnership Firm

  • PAN of company.
  • Address proof of company.
  • Partnership deed and LLP agreement.

Types of MSME Registration

Provision MSME Registration

This certificate is permitted to the firms during the pre-investment period. It has the following benefits,

  • Facilitates ease to get approvals, accommodation, and land.
  • Ease of obtaining clearances and NOC.
Permanent MSME Registration

It is granted to the firm which exists already. It has the below-mentioned benefits,

  • Exemption from tax.
  • The preference is given to the purchase and price.
