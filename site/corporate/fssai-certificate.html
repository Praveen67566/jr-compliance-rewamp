FSSAI Certification Overview

The certification from FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) assures the customer that the safety and quality of food items enforce adherence to rules that safeguard consumers from foodborne diseases. Securing this FSSAI certification adds to promoting the credibility of businesses, meeting legal obligations, and building trust among consumers, ultimately contributing to a more secure food chain.
Significance of compliance
Following FSSAI standards ensures the well-being of consumers and verifies the legal status of your food products.
Improves market interest
FSSAI certification increases consumers' trust, making your products more attractive in a competitive market.
Legal security
Compliance with FSSAI regulations shields businesses from potential legal complications associated with food safety breaches.

Benefits of FSSAI

Legal Compliance

Every food business in India is required to adhere to this mandatory compliance, which helps ensure that they follow the Indian food standard to avoid any type of fine or business shutdown.Â 

Enhanced Consumer Trust

Obtaining this certificate builds trust in consumers that the business practices safe and quality food product production and distribution, making them more likely to choose their products over competitors.

Market Expansion Opportunities

Many retailers and institutional buyers require the suppliers to hold FSSAI certification before entering into a contract. This certification can facilitate business expansion for companies to reach a broader audience.

Improved Brand Reputation

Possessing an FSSAI registration enhances a business's reputation in the market. This facilitates differentiation of business from its competitors while at the same time increasing customer trust and loyalty.

FAQ

BIS

FAQ

What is FSSAI registration?
FSSAI certification is a mandatory requirement for food businesses in India to ensure food safety and quality standards.
Who needs FSSAI registration?
All food businesses in India, including manufacturers, packers, importers, distributors, and retailers, must obtain an FSSAI license to deal with food products in India.
What is the cost of FSSAI registration?
The cost of an FSSAI license varies based on the size of the business and the type of license. Basic registration has less fees, whereas state and central licenses have higher fees due to extensive requirements.
Can I sell food online without FSSAI registration?
No, all online food businesses still require an FSSAI license to ensure compliance with food safety standards and safety regulations before selling products to consumers.
What happens if I operate without a license/registration?
Failure to obtain FSSAI certification will result in legal penalties, product recalls, and business closure.
