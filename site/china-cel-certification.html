CEL Certification - A Certificate of Energy Efficiency

CEL certificate indicates energy efficiency of a product, thus, an obligatory requirement to enter the Chinese market. Contact our experts to know all about CEL certification.

What is CEL?

The China Energy Level (CEL) refers to the energy consumption label implemented on the Chinese products, it is very similar to the BEE in India. Moreover, like BEE, every product utilizing electricity needs to have a CEL label implemented on the product to reflect that a product is energy efficient. 

The fundamental reason for the introduction of CEL is to encourage the energy saving of a product, thus, providing energy saving electronic products to the final consumers.

To manufacturer an energy efficient product by meeting the established requrements, contact our experienced and professional team, without which it is not possible to sell or distribute a product in Chinese market.

CEL Certification Process

The application will be duly filed.

Product testing will be conducted in an accredited laboratory.

If a product complies with the standards, allocation of label will be done.

The label will be registered with the authority.

Like other Chinese certification - NAL, CCC, SRRC, and more, CEL certification is one of the obligatory requirement to enter the Chinese market. As it indicates that a product is safe, reliable and high-quality. While as important as it is to enter the market, its certification process is quite complex too. Because any inaccuracy in documentation, technical aspects of a product, not meeting established safety standards, and more could result in rejection.
