IEC/ EN 62368-1 Implementation

ICE/ EN 6238- 1 Implementation - the requirements which ensures that a product is safe for the consumer, is an obligatory requirements to enter the European market. Contact our experts to know all about ICE/ EN 6238- 1 Implementation.

What is IEC/ EN 62368-1 Implementation?

At unprecedented rates, electronic and electrical products have been increasing continuously, keeping the consistent change of technology in mind, a new ICE/ EN 62368-1 safety standard has been introduced with the motive to replace the IEC 60950-1 and IEC 60065. 

ICE/ EN 62368-1 standard is a performance-oriented standard which is applicable on the Information Technology Equipment and Audio/ Video Equipment. 

The major reasons for replacing other standards with ICE/ EN 62368-1 were emphasizing on the old perspective approaches. Thus, keeping the changes in the technology in mind, standards have been revised and changed.

In a nutshell, it’s fundamental motive is to address appropriate safety problems to ensure adequate product design, production, and market placement.

Does the ICE/ EN 62368-1 standard include product testing?

ICE/ EN 62368-1 standard includes product testing, some of the product testing are similar to that of the IEC 60950-1 standards. Moreover, it might be possible to adapt the test report from IEC 60950-1 standards depending on the type of application and laboratory an ITE is working with.
