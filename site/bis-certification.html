Introduction :

BIS Certification, standing for Bureau of Indian Standards Certification, is a mandatory quality assurance process for various products, including electronic devices, in India. Administered by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), a national body responsible for formulating standards and ensuring product quality and safety, BIS Certification holds significant importance for businesses. This article explores the significance of BIS License for electronic products, its importance, and how JR Compliance, a reputable company, can assist businesses in obtaining BIS Certification online.

Understanding BIS Certification :

BIS Registration is a mark of conformity to specific quality standards set by the Indian government. Its primary objective is to protect consumers from substandard products, ensure public safety, and promote fair trade practices. For electronic items like smartphones, laptops, tablets, and other appliances sold in India, BIS Authorization is a legal requirement.

Significance of BIS Certification

Quality Assurance: BIS Certification guarantees that electronic products meet prescribed standards, ensuring superior quality and optimal performance that satisfies customer expectations.

Safety Compliance: BIS Certification subjects electronic devices to rigorous safety testing, reducing the risk of potential hazards and ensuring consumer safety.

Legal Compliance: BIS Certification is necessary for various electronic products as per Indian law compliance. Those who fall in the category of Non-compliance, are not permittable to run their businesses smoothly and might even witness product seizure or even lead to pay high amount of penalties.   


Market Access: BIS Certification opens doors to the vast Indian market, giving businesses a competitive edge and fostering consumer trust, leading to increased sales and market share.

BIS Certification for Electronic Products

BIS Certification holds particular importance in India's rapidly growing electronics market. Under the Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS), electronic products such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, power banks, LED lamps, printers, and other appliances require BIS Certification before legal sale within the country.

What is the difference between ISI Certification and ISI FMCS?

Under FMCS, a certificate is given to a foreign manufacturer to use the standard mark on the products that comply with Indian Standards. While ISI certification reflects that a domestic product abides by the Indian standards.

Moreover, the FMCS certificate has broad-based because it is a completely manual process, including activities such as off-shore traveling. Yet, its importance can not be overstated to sell products in the Indian market, like ISI certification.

Hence, both certifications are essential for the manufacturing and import of the products in India, if they fail to comply with the Indian standards, the manufacturer has to face credibility issues, trust issues, competitor disadvantages, etc.

In the case of mandatory products, if the manufacturer fails to obtain the ISI certification, the manufacturer is liable for a fine or imprisonment, both. 

Thus, its benefits are not limited to competitor advantages but it can also grant you legal advantages, and wider market access.

Why is BIS certification important?

BIS certification not only holds importance for customers’ safety but also provides a legal advantage to the manufacturer, especially if the product falls under the purview of BIS.


Yet, if manufacturers fail to obtain the BIS certification for the mandatory products, they have to face debarment for 2 years, a fine, not less than Rs 2 lakh, or both.


Moreover, a BIS license is essential to obtain to avail legal benefits, competitive advantages, quality of product, and easy market access.

Who needs a BIS Certificate?

BIS Registration Certificate is essentially granted to Manufacturing Units. However, traders and importers of eligible products can also secure this certification in the name of their respective Manufacturing Units.

What is the use of a BIS Certificate?

Product certification as per the BIS is aimed to guarantee safety, quality, and reliability to third parties. The certification aims to ensure product conformity as per the Indian Standards (IS).

What is the validity of the BIS Certificate?

BIS registration remains valid for the duration of two years, and it is renewal in nature. The authority seeks a few documents and prescribed fees for this purpose. On-site or lab testing is not required to renew the BIS certification.

Is BIS Certification mandatory for gold?

Yes, given the exemption cited in the compulsory hallmarking order, a jeweller with a yearly turnover of less than INR 40 lacs may sell hallmarked jewellery in locations under mandatory hallmarking if they have a certificate of registration from BIS.
