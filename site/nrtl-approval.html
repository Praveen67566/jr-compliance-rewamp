What is NRTL?

A Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory (NRTL) is an independent organization which certifies that a product meets the established standards, thus, can be sold or distributed in the USA and Canada. 

In  a nutshell, NRTL certification is necessary to enter the USA and Canada market because it serves as evidence that an electric and electronic product has complied with the established standards. Therefore, a product is safe for the final consumers, including it provides wider market and competitive advantages.

Each NRTL includes a scope of test standards which includes its own unique registration certification mark.

Who is Eligible To Obtain NRTL Certification

As mentioned, NRTL is an private or independent organization recognized by OSHA to conduct product testing and certification. Below-mentioned are the eligibility requirements -

Ability to test and evaluate equipment to comply with adequate test standards.

Availability of appropriate controls to identify certified products, including conducting proper follow-ups inspections for actual production. 

An effective procedure must be followed for generating its findings and to handle complaints and disputes.

A complete independence from the manufacturer, user, or vendor of the product of certified product.

These requirements are necessary to obtain OSHA NRTL for both - product safety testing laboratory and as a product certification body.

How to Get NRTL Certification?

An application will be duly submitted for an initial recognition by OSHA.

Once it is demonstrated that established criteria and requirements have been met through evaluation and reviewing of each application materials.

In the case of foreign applicants, OSHA must consider the reciprocity of the concerned government.

If an organization has met the criteria, an in-depth on-site inspection will be conducted.

Thereafter, OSHA will publish a notice to the Federal Register for public comment.

Following the 30 days after publishing of the notice, a final decision will be made in regard to the comments in the Federal Register. 

An NRTL certificate will be issued if the product has met established standards.
