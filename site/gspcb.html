What is Goa State Pollution State?

It is an autonomous statutory organization established on July 1, 1988 under Water and Air Act, 1974. However, before the formation of Goa State Pollution Board, the Central Board for the Prevention and Control of Water Pollution was performing its functions. For this purpose, the Central Pollution Board Ponda-Goa had been established as a Section Office.

Objectives of Goa State Pollution Control Board

Controlling the pollution from its source considering the techno- economic feasibility for liquid effluents and gases emissions.
Prevent polluting the water resources through the discharge of untreated city sewage.
Encouraging the reuse or recycling of sewage or trade effluent on land for irrigation and for industrial motive after proper treatment.
Reducing the pollution control requirements by judicious location of the new industries and relocation, as required.

How to Obtain CTE/ CTO Under GSPCB?

An online application will be filed.

Receiving officer will scrutinize the application depending on the complete/ in-complete submission of the application.

An inspection will be conducted by a Regional Officer (RO).

A Consent will either be issued by RO or your application will move forward to Unit Head.

The application moved forward will be scrutinized by Unit Head or Consent will be issued, or an application will move forward to Member Secretary.

The Member Secretary will issue the consent.
