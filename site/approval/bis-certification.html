BIS Overview

In India, the national standards body for products and services is the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), which is in charge of establishing and overseeing quality standards. BIS certification ensures that products meet Indian safety and quality standards, facilitating market access and consumer trust. It plays a crucial role in protecting consumer interests, promoting fair trade practices, and supporting the growth of industries by establishing consistent quality benchmarks.

Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS) provides a means for products manufactured out of India to meet Indian quality and safety standards for the products to be sold in the Indian market. This certification is mandatory for products imported in India like electrical goods, automotive parts, and consumer appliances.

FAQ

What is BIS certification, and why is it required?
Products with BIS certification must adhere to Indian standards for quality, safety, and reliability to ensure consumer protection. Before being sold in India, certain items such as electronics, cement, and electrical appliances must undergo this mandatory certification process.
Which products require mandatory BIS certification in India?
Mandatory BIS certification applies to a variety of products such as electrical appliances, electronics, cement, and consumer goods, guaranteeing compliance with Indian safety standards and bolstering consumer confidence in product quality.
How long does the BIS certification process usually take?
The BIS certification typically requires 4-6 weeks to complete, varying based on the intricacy of the product, testing, and documentation needs. Utilizing a consultant can expedite the process, leading to faster and more seamless certification approvals.
Can BIS certification be applied to imported products in India?
Yes, before being sold in India, imported goods that need to be certified legally must also have BIS certification.
