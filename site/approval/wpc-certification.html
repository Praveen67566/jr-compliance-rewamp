Types of approvals in WPC certification

Equipment Type Approval (ETA) | ETA Approval

ETA is required for importing, selling, or using such equipment in India for wireless devices operating in de-licensed frequency bands, ensuring compliance with national standards.

Demonstration License

This license allows companies to showcase wireless products at exhibitions, trade fairs, or client demonstrations essential for marketing, testing, and client interactions.

Experimental License

Issued for research and development, this license permits organizations to test new wireless technologies before commercialization. It ensures controlled experimentation without disrupting licensed frequencies.

Internet Service Provider (ISP) License

Required by telecom companies, it enables lawful wireless broadband distribution while maintaining national security and communication standards.

Dealer Possession License (DPL)

Wireless device dealers require this license to legally trade, stock, and distribute wireless equipment. Essential for resellers and importers, DPL ensures only certified dealers handle wireless communication products within the Indian market.

Non-Dealer Possession License (NDPL)

NDPL is issued to individuals or organizations possessing wireless equipment for personal or operational use without resale intentions. It prevents unauthorized possession, ensuring compliance with spectrum regulations.

Import License

This license ensures that imported devices adhere to Indian frequency standards, preventing unauthorized or harmful radio interference.Â

FAQ

BIS

FAQ

What is WPC certification for wireless devices?
WPC certification ensures that wireless devices operating in de-licensed frequency bands comply with Indian telecom regulations. It is mandatory for manufacturers and importers to sell such devices legally.
How long does the WPC certification process take?
The timeline varies based on application accuracy and regulatory workload, typically ranging from 2 to 4 weeks. Ensuring complete documentation speeds up the process.
Is WPC certification mandatory for imported wireless devices?
Yes, all imported wireless devices operating in de-licensed bands require WPC certification before entering the Indian market.
What documents are needed for WPC certification?
Key documents include the application form, RF reports, test results, and authorization proof if filing through a consultant.
Can I apply for WPC certification myself?
While self-application is possible, hiring experts ensures accurate documentation and avoids procedural delays, expediting approval.
