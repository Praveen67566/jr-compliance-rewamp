Scheme-X under BIS in India ensures that products meet the required quality, safety, and performance standards. It is a mandatory certification for manufacturers producing goods that fall under BIS regulations. By obtaining Scheme-X certification, businesses can enhance credibility, ensure consumer safety, and seamlessly enter Indian markets while complying with government mandates.

Service Overview

Scheme-X under BIS focuses on streamlining the certification of goods through standard compliance procedures. It provides a structured framework where manufacturers must meet BIS standards before selling their products in India. The certification enhances consumer confidence, ensures legal market entry, and demonstrates a brand's commitment to delivering high-quality, safe, and reliable products.

Extensive Documentation and Approvals
Complying with Scheme-X requires detailed documentation, test reports, manufacturing details, and other proofs, making the initial application process lengthy and complex for businesses.
Frequent BIS Standard Updates
Manufacturers must stay updated with constant amendments in BIS standards, which may require re-testing or recertification to maintain compliance under Scheme-X regulations.
Technical Testing Complexity
Under Scheme-X, product samples undergo rigorous testing in BIS-recognized labs, and ensuring accurate results without technical errors can be challenging for first-time applicants.
Higher Cost for Non-Compliance
Delays or non-compliance in following Scheme-X processes can result in penalties, mandatory product recalls, or loss of market access, severely impacting business profitability.

Competitive Advantage in the Industry

Ensures Consumer Safety and Satisfaction

Service Process

01
Application Preparation and Submission
Prepare necessary documents, test reports, and technical data, and submit them through the official BIS portal to initiate Scheme-X certification.
02
Product Testing and Sampling
Samples are collected and tested in BIS-recognized laboratories to verify compliance with the required standards applicable under Scheme-X regulations.
03
Factory Inspection and Audit
BIS officials conduct an inspection of the manufacturing unit to evaluate quality control measures, production capabilities, and compliance with prescribed guidelines.
04
Evaluation of Test Reports and Audit
BIS authorities assess the test results and audit findings to ensure that the product consistently meets quality and safety standards.
05
Grant of Certification under Scheme-X
Upon successful evaluation, BIS grants the Scheme-X certification, allowing manufacturers to use the Standard Mark on their certified products.
06
Post-Certification Surveillance Activities
Periodic surveillance audits, re-testing, and factory inspections are conducted to maintain compliance with BIS standards under Scheme-X certification.

FAQ

What is Scheme-X under BIS certification?\
Scheme-X under BIS is a mandatory certification scheme ensuring that products meet Indian standards for quality, safety, and performance before market entry.
Is Scheme-X certification mandatory for all products?
No, Scheme-X is mandatory only for products listed under the BIS Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) or specific regulatory notifications.
How long does it take to obtain Scheme-X certification?
Generally, the process takes between 30 to 90 working days, depending on documentation, factory inspections, and laboratory testing timelines.
What happens if a product fails Scheme-X testing?
If a product fails BIS testing, the application may be rejected or delayed, requiring manufacturers to rectify issues and resubmit samples.
Can foreign manufacturers apply for Scheme-X?
Yes, foreign manufacturers can apply through an Authorized Indian Representative (AIR) to obtain Scheme-X certification for products sold in India.
