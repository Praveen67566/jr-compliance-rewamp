What is Punjab State Pollution Control Board?

The Punjab State Pollution Control Board (PPCB) established in 1975 under the vide vide Punjab Government Notification No. 6186-BR II (4) 75/24146 dated 30.07.1975, subsequent to the enactment of Water Act, 1974. However, after that, PPCB has been entrusted with more responsibility to implement the provisions under environmental laws.

Some of the provisions PPCB follows are -

  • The Water (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 as amended to date
  • The Water (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Cess Act, 1977
  • The Air (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, 1981 as amended to date
  • The Hazardous Waste (Management & Handling) Rules, 1989 as amended to date
  • And other environmental laws and regulations

Objectives of PPCB

Control the pollution at its source.
Prevent the pollution of natural waters.
Lessen the use of trade effluents and sewage, including encouraging the appropriate treatment of the same.
Lessening the pollution control requirements by minimizing the reuse or recycling.
Encouraging the reuse and recycling of sewage and trade effluent.
Collecting and compilation of the statistical/ technical data in respect to air and water pollution.
Creating public awareness regarding cleanliness and a healthy environment.
Coordinating and rapport with Industries Association, Government Organizations, etc to create environment awareness and ensure compliance.
Ensure prompt clearance of approvals.

How to Obtain CTE/ CTO Under PPCB?

An online application will be filed.

Receiving officer will scrutinize the application depending on the complete/ in-complete submission of the application.

An inspection will be conducted by a Regional Officer (RO).

A Consent will either be issued by RO or your application will move forward to Unit Head.

The application moved forward will be scrutinized by Unit Head or Consent will be issued, or an application will move forward to Member Secretary.

The Member Secretary will issue the consent.
