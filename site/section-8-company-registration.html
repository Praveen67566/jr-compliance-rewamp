How to do a Section 8 Company Registration in India?

Section 8 company registration in India involves several steps to comply with the legal requirements under the Indian Companies Act, 2013. Here’s a detailed guide:

1. Select a Unique Name: Choose a unique name for your company that aligns with the naming guidelines provided by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).

2 . Obtain Digital Signatures: Acquire Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) for all proposed directors to facilitate the electronic filing of forms.

3. Apply for DIN: Ensure all proposed directors have a Director Identification Number (DIN). If not, apply for DIN for each director.

4 . Draft MOA and AOA: Prepare the Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA), outlining the company’s objectives and governing rules.

5. File Incorporation Forms: Submit the necessary forms, such as Form INC-12 for the license and Form INC-7 for incorporation, along with the required documents, to the MCA.

6. Obtain a Section 8 License: The Registrar of Companies (ROC) will review your application and, if satisfied, issue a license under Section 8.

7. Receive a Certificate of Incorporation: Once the ROC approves, you will receive the Certificate of Incorporation, officially registering your company.

8 . Post-Incorporation Compliance: Adhere to ongoing statutory requirements, including maintaining financial records, conducting regular board meetings, and filing annual returns.

9 . By following these steps, you can successfully complete a Section 8 company registration in India, enabling you to focus on your non-profit goals.

There is no underlying limitation over section 8 entities pertaining to minimum capital requirement, unlike other companies.

A section 8 Company is not liable to pay stamp duty for registration.

A section 8 company holds its own legal identity just like other registered companies & rejoices autonomous legal standing from its members.

A Section 8 Entity is more credible in terms of compliances and legal standing

Section 8 companies have the leverage to choose the name that suits their liking during the registration process.

Documents required for Section 8 Company Incorporation Process:

  • Articles of Association (aka AOA) & Memorandum of Association (MOA)
  • Consent by the first director(s) as well as a subscriber(s) (Affidavit not required) under form no. DIR2 and subscriber sheet respectively.
  • Proof of office address
  • INC-14
  • INC-15
  • NOC from the owner of the premises and copy of utility bills such as electricity bills, water bills, or gas bills.
  • Resident and identity proof of directors.
  • DSC of all directors.
  • Projected income expenditure.
  • Grounds of application.
