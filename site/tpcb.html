Tripura State Pollution Control Board

CPCB has delegate its powers to SPCB and Tripura State Pollution Control Board is one of the SPCB responsible for the implementation of several laws to control or prevent pollution in different sectors.

Contact JR Compliance for reliable, flexible, and adpatable services.

What is the Tripura State Pollution Control Board?

Tripura is a state in northeast India and the third-smallest State of India, which is established in the geographically isolated location with the rich resources. And to prevent the environmental degradation, Tripura State Pollution Control Board has been established depending on Water Act, 1974.

Thereafter, Air Act, 1981, and other environmental laws, rules, and regulations have been enacted, that further widened the responsibility of TSPCB, that is to prevent and control the pollution.

Objectives of Tripura State Pollution Control Board

Monitoring whether compliance requirements are being followed or not.
Regulate and control noise generating from sources.
To establish, modify, or annual effluent standards for sewage and trade effluent.
Prevent polluting the water resources through the discharge of untreated city sewage.
Collecting and disseminating information in regard to prevention, control, and abatement of pollution, thereof.
Issuance of NOC to concerned industries.
To conduct inspection of the manufacturing process, control equipment, etc.
Closing default industries.

How to Obtain CTE/ CTO ?

An online application will be filed.

Receiving officer will scrutinize the application depending on the complete/ in-complete submission of the application.

An inspection will be conducted by a Regional Officer (RO).

A Consent will either be issued by RO or your application will move forward to Unit Head.

The application moved forward will be scrutinized by Unit Head or Consent will be issued, or an application will move forward to Member Secretary.

The Member Secretary will issue the consent.
