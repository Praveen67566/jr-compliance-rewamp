Benefits of Mutual Funds

Provide Means to Earn Money

Below-mentioned are the ways through which Mutuals funds helps in earning money,

Capital Gain Distributions

When an individual sells the high-priced securities, that person can avail of the high capital gains. Moreover, at the end of the year, the investors will receive the fund distributions excluding capital losses.


Dividend Payments

The fund probably earns the income from the dividend on stocks or interest on bonds. Consequently, these funds pay nearly the total income of shareholders. 


High Net Asset Value

After excluding the expenses, if the value of the fund’s portfolio exceeds, it will increase the value of funds and its shares.

Flexibility in Liquidity

Mutual funds allow investors to redeem their money anytime. Therefore, unlike Fixed Deposit, mutual funds provide pliable withdrawals. However, before that, you have to consider factors such as pre-exit penalty and exit load.

Expert Management

In mutual funds, the experts combine their money and allocate it in various securities. Thus, assist the investors in incurring profit. Moreover, experts also keep a check on challenges and provide the most appropriate solution for the same.

Therefore, it allows the experts to use their expertise and knowledge to manage and operate the mutual fund.

Ensures Diversification

The value of investment won’t increase or decrease in tandem. So, if the value of an investment has increased, the value of others probably will decrease. Hence, the overall performance value of a portfolio has minimum potential to be volatile.

In simpler words, diversification minimizes the risk involved in creating the portfolio, consequently minimizing the investor’s risk.

Pliability to Invest in Small Quantity

Mutual funds allow you to invest according to the cash flow position. This eliminates the need to invest a considerable amount of capital at once.

Ensures Safety and Transparency

With the introduction of SEBI guidelines, each mutual fund is labeled, which provides color-coding to all the Mutual Fund Schemes. Thus, an investor can ascertain the level of risk in the investment. Hence, it makes the investment process crystalline.

Low Tax Gains

Under Section 80C of the Income Tax (IT) Act, with the help of an equity-linked saving scheme, an individual can save up to Rs 1.5 lakh per year.

Types of Mutuals Funds

1. Money Market Funds

Money market funds involve the lowest risk. These funds are short term instruments, generally government Treasury funds. As it offers low risk, the substantial return is also low.

2. Equity Funds

As the name suggests, this kind of fund fundamentally invests in stocks. Additionally, equity funds are divided into various subcategories depending on the company size, investment style of holding the portfolio, and geography. 

3. Index Fund

An index fund is a combination of stocks and bonds designed to imitate the composition and performance of the financial market index.

4. Fixed Income Fund

A fixed-income fund is one of the most significant categories of funds. As its name implies, it provides returns at fixed intervals (monthly, quarterly, or yearly). However, the income depends on the individual rate, not on the fixed amount.

5. Balanced Fund

The balanced fund includes the components of stocks and bonds. The objective of the balanced fund is to minimize the risk of exposure across assets classes. 

6. Income Fund

As the name suggests, the income fund provides steady current income. Thereto primarily focus on current income, either monthly or quarterly, against capital gains or appreciation.

7. Specialty Fund

Specialty fund is more of every category as it invests in energy companies, financial sector, technology, health, etc.

8. International or Global Fund

As the name suggests, the international fund only invests in foreign assets. However, global funds can provide support worldwide, including in domestic countries.

9. Exchange Traded Fund

It tracks the index, commodity, and other assets purchased and sold on a stock exchange, like a regular stock.

Eligibility Criteria For Registration Under SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulation, 1996

The sponsor should not be engaged in any illegal activity. Thus, a sponsor should have a soundtrack record. Here, “soundtrack” refers to,

  1. Should continue to engage in financial services for at least five years.
  2. The net worth of the immediate previous year must be more than the capital contribution of the sponsor in asset management establishment.
  3. The net worth of the company must be positive in every five previous years.
  4. The sponsor must have profit excluding depreciation, interest, and tax in three out of five immediate previous years, comprising the fifth year.

In existing mutual funds, such funds are present in trust, and the trust deed needs approval.

The candidate/applicant should be a fit and proper person.

The sponsor must contribute at least 40% and above to the net worth of the asset management company.

The sponsor/director/principal officer should not engage in fraud or be convicted for any offense, including immorality or economic offense.

You are required to appoint a custodian to keep the custody of securities and carry the custodian activities as prescribed by trustees.

You are required to appoint the trustees, as they will act as the trustees for mutual funds as per provisions of regulations.

To manage the mutual funds and operate the scheme of these funds, you must appoint the asset management company as per provisions of regulations.

List of every associate or group companies or subsidiaries registered under SEBI, including the registered capacity and SEBI registration number.
Information regarding cases of suspension and cancellation of registration certificate of sponsor/associates of sponsor must be disclosed for the previous ten years.
Any sponsor/its group or associate companies is listed under any recognized stock exchange in India.
In the case of foreign entities and monetary penalties in Indian entities, mention the top 10 monetary penalties imposed.

Information concerning company or associate or group companies registered or need registration under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) as a banking or non-banking company or in any capacity.

How to Start Mutual Fund Business? (Registration Process)

Fill the registration form A as prescribed under Schedule-I of SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations,1996. To be noted, according to the proviso to Registration 7 (c) of the Regulations, any individual possessing 40% or above net worth of assets in an asset management company must deem to be a sponsor and need to fill the Form A.

After this, affix the required information and documents.

In the case of an existing establishment, SEBI will conduct an on-site examination with objectives of,

  1. To make the record of handling complaints or grievances.
  2. The previous infrastructure of customer servicing and handling of complaints.
  3. Philosophy and practice of compliance.

Further, within 30 days from the date of communication receipt from SEBI, you must provide all required information. However, failing to provide the necessary information, your application will be considered closed.

If the communication receipt states that you are eligible, you are required to follow the below-mentioned steps within 12 months from the date of communication,

  1. Auditor’s certificate.
  2. Incorporation of Asset Management of Company along with Trustees Company or Board of Trustees.
  3. Developing the infrastructure by the candidate or applicant.
  4. Replica of trust deed and Investment Management Agreement.

Lastly, as you complete the requirements as mentioned earlier, you will obtain the certification of registration.

Note- If no mutual fund scheme is made within 12 months of the registration date, the grant of the registration certificate will be canceled.
