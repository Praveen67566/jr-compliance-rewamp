SRRC Certificate - A Certificate to Enter the Chinese Market

Obtain SRRC certificate for equipment which uses radio transmitting functions to create a safe environment worldwide and to enter the Chinese market. Contact our experts to know how to get a SRRC type approval and Modular approval under SRRC.

What is an SRRC Certificate?

State Radio Regulatory Commission (SRRC) is an authority which is responsible for providing certificates to the appliances and equipment which have radio transmitting functions. 

Like CCC, NAL, CEL, and other certifications of China, SRRC is one of the certificates which is obligatory to obtain to sell or distribute products in the China market. 

SRRC includes different types of approval -

  • SRRC type approval
  • Modular approval

Under SRRC type approval, each model needs to be certified, however, modular approval allows the series of models to be certified together. On the contrary, in the case of a particular category of end products, a modular approval might negate the requirement of testing and certification.

SRRC Certification Process

Application will be duly filed.

Product or module will be sent for testing in an accredited laboratory.

In case of module approval, a test board needs to be available to operate in a test module mode.

If a product or module has met established standards, an SRRC certificate will be issued.

Product testing is an important part of SRRC certification as it reflects that a product has met the safety requirements. After testing, the test report is viewed and analyzed by the SRRC authorities to determine whether a product meets requirements or not. 

Depending on which, a SRRC certificate is provided to a manufacturer. 

Indeed, it requires a great deal of effort and understanding of each and every aspect of the SRRC certification process to ensure accuracy.
