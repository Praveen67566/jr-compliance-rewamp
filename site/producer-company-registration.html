What is a Producer Company?

Under the Companies Act, 2013, a Producer Company was introduced, which provides an opportunity to the individuals engaged in producing activities (particularly by farming) to form a company.

Moreover, a producer company is a corporate entity whose objectives/activities are described in Section 518B.

Does a Producer Company Receive Tax Benefits?

Under Section 10(1) of the Income Tax Act, agricultural incomes are not subject to pay tax, yet, sometimes the exemptions are present, depending on the execution of the farming activities.

Moreover, its definition does not define any particular unique tax benefit or exemptions that a producer company avails. However, concerning agricultural activities that the producer company executes, certain tax benefits and exemptions can be availed. 

For instance, under the Income Tax Act, income generated from selling the grown tea leaves is counted as agricultural income and is completely tax-free. On the contrary, if the same tea leaves are processed for manufacturing, then 60% of the income from such activity will be considered agriculture income. However, 40% will be taxed.

Thus, the tax benefit and exemption depending on the activities of a producer company.

Benefits of Producer Company

Limited Liability

The members of the producer company have limited liability which means that the personal property of its members would be safe, unlike a sole proprietorship where the business owner is liable to pay all the debts and liabilities.

Appropriate Management

Unlike a sole proprietorship, a producer company ensures appropriate company management as the entire business and work are divided into directors.

Separate Legal Existence

A separate legal entity is an organization that continues to exist despite the death, bankruptcy, egress, etc., of the members of the society.

Economies of Scale

A producer company provides an opportunity to the farmers to work collectively and at a lower cost and minimize the risk. Also, it provides access to appropriate credit facilities.

Types of Producer Companies

1. Production Business

Production business engages in manufacturing, production, or procurement of the primary output for the members of the producer company for further sale.

2. Infrastructure Business

It is the businesses that are engaged in providing infrastructure to their members. It could be in the form of water resources, electricity, land utilization, irrigation techniques, or consultation concerning the same.

3. Marketing Business

A business that engages in marketing and promotional activities of primary produce or provision of educational services to its members and others can establish a producer company.

4. Financing Business

Any business involved in the producer’s financing activities, whether in production, development, or marketing, can form a producer company.

5. Technical Business

A business provides technical help to producers and provides training and educational services; conducting research and development can form a producer company.

Objectives of a Producer Company

Should encourage mutuality and mutual assistance techniques.

It provides benefits of production, procurement, harvesting, grading, pooling, marketing, handling, selling, export of primary produce of its members, and importing goods and services.

Should provide the training on principles of mutual assistance to its members.

The manufacturing, sale, or supply using the machinery, equipment, or consumables should chiefly go to its members.

A producer company should never convert to a public company, yet, it can be converted into a multistate cooperative society.
