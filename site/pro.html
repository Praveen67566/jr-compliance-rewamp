1. EPR Registration

EPR is a registration completed under Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) for E-Waste channelization. Basically, it is a mandatory requirement which is applicable to all manufacturer, importer, producer, consumer, bulk consumer, dealers, e-retailer involved in manufacture, sale, transfer, storage and processing of e-waste or electrical and electronic equipment provided in Schedule 1, that further includes their components, consumables and spare parts.

We provide a complete solution for EPR registration, including collection of E-Waste at PAN India level, Dismantling and Recycling. 

Liabilities –

1. CPCB is entrusted with the responsibility to collect and recycle the E-Waste to the Producer itself.
2. Either the producer can fulfill the responsibility by itself or can engage a PRO (Producer’s Responsibility Organization) for fulfillment of his responsibilities. However, the PRO engaged in collection of e-waste must be CPCB authorized.
3. The producer (Manufacturer/Importer) is required to submit the data of previous import/sales data in accordance to which the targets will be allotted by CPCB. Further, once EPR certificate is issued, the quarterly import/sales data needs to be submitted.
4. The producer needs to conduct 2 seminars yearly to create public awareness regarding the hazardous effects of E-waste, including methods to properly channelize and dispose of E-waste in an eco-friendly manner.

Details  –

EPR is a registration through which the government has given an extended responsibility to the producer to channelize the E-waste generated into the markets. Producers are liable to collect back electronics through buy-back schemes, collection programmes and collection centers.

Proper channelization is important so that e-waste doesn’t end up in the wrong hands and harm the environment. Improper handling and hazardous dumping has degraded the environment and has impacted human health. Producers have to take the responsibility for proper channelization and have to get it recycled from an authorized recycler. General public should also be addressed for proper disposal of e-waste.

2. Compliance

JR Compliance and Testing Lab provides assistance to producers, importers and manufacturers of electronics and electrical equipment (EEE) to fulfill their Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) obligations under the E-Waste (Management) Rules 2016.

While providing assistance we also ensure to provide end-to-end services to the Producers with the purpose to become EPR compliant. With that, we consist of a large reverse logistics network in PAN India that connects stakeholders across the value chain including consumers, bulk consumers, waste pickers, and aggregators.

Following are the Compliances of EPR E-Waste Authorization

A producer after obtaining an EPR Authorization is required to fulfill below-mentioned compliance requirements which are termed as EPR Compliances.

  • Creating awareness in respect to E-Waste through -
  1. Media
  2. Publications
  3. Newspaper
  4. Posters
  5. Awareness program
  6. Seminar
  7. Digital Promotion
  • Providing information regarding  address, e-mail address, toll-free telephone numbers, etc.
  • Providing information regarding  hazardous constituents.
  • Providing information on hazards of improper handling, disposal, accidental breakage, etc.
  • Providing instructions for handling and disposal of the equipment after its use.
